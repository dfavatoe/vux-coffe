	1	The café’s existing one-page website (URL, screenshots, and/or source code will be provided below)

Your output must be a fully implemented landing page, not just design suggestions.

UX / UI Requirements
UX Principles
	•	Extremely clear information hierarchy
	•	Fast cognitive scanning
	•	Pleasant rhythm while scrolling
	•	Mobile-first design
	•	Accessible contrast and font sizing
	•	Intuitive CTA placement (menu, visit, opening hours, Instagram)
UI Style
	•	Minimalist but emotionally charged
	•	Strong typography (serif + sans or mono combination encouraged)
	•	Neutral palette with one or two accent colors
	•	Intentional white space
	•	Micro-interactions (hover states, transitions) — subtle, not flashy
	•	Modern but not boring

Content Strategy
Use the Instagram content to:

You may:

Required Sections (You may reorder or refine)
